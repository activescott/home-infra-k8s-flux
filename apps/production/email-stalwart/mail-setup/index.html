Set up your mail

Mailboxes on mail.activescott.com.

1. Create an app password

Accounts here use two-factor authentication, and mail apps cannot do two-factor logins. Each app needs its own app password instead of your normal one.

  1. Sign in at admin.mail.activescott.com.
  2. Open Account in the sidebar, then App Passwords.
  3. Create one, name it after the device (“iPhone”), and copy it.

It is shown once. Save it in your password manager. Make a separate one per device so you can revoke a lost phone without touching anything else.

2. iPhone, iPad or Mac

Install the configuration profile. It fills in the server names and ports, which Apple Mail otherwise guesses wrong.

Install the profile
  1. Tap the button. It downloads rather than opening.
  2. Open Settings. Near the top: Profile Downloaded. On a Mac, it is System Settings › General › Device Management.
  3. Tap Install. It is unsigned, so you get a warning — expected.
  4. Enter your full email address when asked, and the app password from step 1.

Removing the profile later removes the mail account with it.

Any other mail app

Enter these by hand. Use your full email address as the username in both.

Incoming IMAP, mail.activescott.com, port 993, SSL/TLS on
Outgoing SMTP, mail.activescott.com, port 465, SSL/TLS on
Password the app password from step 1, not your sign-in password

Port 587 does not work here — nothing listens on it. Apps that pick their own ports often land on 587 or 25 and then report a wrong password. Set 465 explicitly.
