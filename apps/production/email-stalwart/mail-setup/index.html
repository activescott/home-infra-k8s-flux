Set up your mail

Mail, calendar and contacts on mail.activescott.com.

1. Create an app password

Accounts here use two-factor authentication, and mail apps cannot do two-factor logins. Each device needs its own app password instead of your normal one.

  1. Sign in at admin.mail.activescott.com.
  2. Open Account in the sidebar, then App Passwords.
  3. Create one, name it after the device (“iPhone”), and copy it.

It is shown once. Save it in your password manager. Make a separate one per device so you can revoke a lost phone without touching anything else.

2. iPhone, iPad or Mac

Enter your details and install the profile. It sets up mail, calendar and contacts in one step, with the server names and ports filled in — which Apple Mail otherwise guesses wrong.

What people see in the From line of your mail. Leave it blank and your address is used instead.

Install the profile
  1. Tap the button. It downloads rather than opening.
  2. Open Settings. Near the top: Profile Downloaded. On a Mac, it is System Settings › General › Device Management.
  3. Tap Install. It is unsigned, so you get a warning — expected.
  4. Enter the app password from step 1. You will be asked three times — once each for mail, calendar and contacts. Same password every time.

Removing the profile later removes all three accounts with it.

3. Deleting mail does not lose it

Your device empties Trash on its own after about a week, and on an account installed from a profile there is no setting to stop it — iOS does not expose Advanced for profile-provisioned accounts at all. The swipe action stays “Trash” too, for the same reason.

That is fine here, because the server keeps the copy instead of relying on the client. Every message delivered to you is filed into Archive as well as the Inbox — not a second copy, the same message in two places. Deleting it from the Inbox, and your device later emptying the Trash, both just remove it from those mailboxes. It stays in Archive.

So the only way to actually destroy a message is to delete it out of Archive. Everything else is reversible — go and look in Archive, it will be there.

On a Mac, where Mail › Settings › Accounts › Mailbox Behaviours is available, you can set Move discarded messages into: Archive if you prefer archiving to trashing. It changes where mail goes first, not whether it survives.

Any other mail app

Enter these by hand. Use your full email address as the username.

Incoming IMAP, mail.activescott.com, port 993, SSL/TLS on
Outgoing SMTP, mail.activescott.com, port 465, SSL/TLS on
Calendar CalDAV, https://mail.activescott.com/dav/cal
Contacts CardDAV, https://mail.activescott.com/dav/card
Password the app password from step 1, not your sign-in password

Port 587 does not work here — nothing listens on it. Apps that pick their own ports often land on 587 or 25 and then report a wrong password. Set 465 explicitly.
