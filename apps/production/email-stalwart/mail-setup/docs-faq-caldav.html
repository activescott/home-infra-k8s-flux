How do I connect another app to my calendar?

Scheduling tools, task managers and anything else that speaks CalDAV. Three values: a URL, a username, and an app password.

1. Make an app password for that app

Do not give an app your sign-in password. Make it its own, so you can take it back without changing anything else.

  1. Sign in at admin.mail.activescott.com.
  2. Go to Account › App Passwords. Any you made earlier are listed there.
  3. Create one and name it after the app, so a year from now the list still makes sense.
  4. Copy it. It is shown once and never again.

Deleting an app password cuts off that one app immediately and leaves every other device signed in.

2. Give the app these three values

URL https://mail.activescott.com/dav/cal/you%40example.com
Your own address, with the @ written as %40.
Username your full email address, @ and all
Password the app password from step 1

The address appears twice, once in the URL and once as the username. That is correct, not a mistake.

Why %40 and not @

In that URL your address is a folder name, and @ is not an ordinary character in a web address — some apps cut the URL in half at it and then report that the server cannot be found. Writing it as %40 means the same thing and every app handles it.

Contacts, and apps that find things themselves

Address books work the same way with card in place of cal: https://mail.activescott.com/dav/card/you%40example.com.

Some apps ask only for a server, not a full calendar URL. Give those https://mail.activescott.com/ and they will find the rest on their own.

If it will not connect

Rejects the password The username has to be the full email address, and the password has to be an app password — your sign-in password will not work here.
Connects, no calendars The URL is probably missing the address on the end. It should be /dav/cal/you%40example.com, not just /dav/cal.
Nothing loads at all Check the host is mail.activescott.com. The admin. address is only for signing in and making app passwords; calendars are not served from it.

Known to work

Cal.com — Apps › CalDav, which asks for exactly the three values above. It checks them as you save, so if it accepts the connection it really is working; there is no silent half-failure to worry about.

Anything you connect can read and change your calendar. Only connect things you would trust with it, and delete the app password when you stop using one.
